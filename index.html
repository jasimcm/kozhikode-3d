ABOUT THIS MODEL

Every building, road, rail line, river, shoreline and hill is placed from real map data in a single metric grid centred on Kozhikode. Surfaces are generated on top of that geometry.

Detailed 3D covers the city core and coast (13 × 16.6 km), from Beypore and Chaliyam in the south to East Hill and Varakkal in the north, and inland past Medical College. Around it, the district out to the Western Ghats foothills is shown as terrain and satellite imagery.

Where building heights come from

Mapped height or floor count
Estimated from footprint size

Only of buildings carry a height or floor count in the map. The rest are estimated from footprint area and distance from the city centre, so individual heights are plausible, not measured. Tall apartment towers are under-represented.

Real

  • Building footprints and positions
  • Terrain, coastline, rivers, canals and ponds
  • Roads and rail alignments
  • Ground colour, from satellite imagery
  • corporation ward boundaries
  • Sun position for Kozhikode on 15 January

Generated

  • Building heights (see above), roof shapes, wall colours, windows and night lights
  • trees and palms, scattered where the satellite image is green
  • Wave motion, street-light glow
  • Landmark models (beach lighthouse, Mishkal Mosque, Tali Temple, EMS Stadium, the Planetarium, CSI and Mother of God cathedrals, Chaliyam lighthouse, sea-pier ruins, a Beypore uru), built by hand and stylised; pier and uru positions are approximate

Limits

  • Ground imagery is 10 m per pixel, so streets blur when you are close.
  • Food-trail and landmark pins use map-listed coordinates (seven food spots were located by a map search); a few may be off by a building or two.
  • Ward boundaries are the 2024–25 delimitation layers as published for public consultation, and may differ from the final notified wards.

Data

  • Buildings, roads, rail, water, places, localities: Overture Maps Foundation release 2026-10-21.0 (includes OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; Google Open Buildings; Microsoft ML Buildings)
  • Terrain: Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM and other open elevation sources)
  • Ground imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2020 by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
  • Ward boundaries: Delimitation Commission, Kerala / Information Kerala Mission (K-SMART ward map)
